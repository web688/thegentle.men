II. Typography & Visual System

The digital broadsheet is set in refined system typefaces to maximize reading speed, battery longevity, and visual harmony:

  • Serif (Headings & Body): Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif. Chosen for its classical contrast, generous optical proportions, and high readability across desktop and mobile screens.
  • Sans-Serif (Navigation & Metadata): -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif. Used with open letter-spacing for crisp archival datelines and UI controls.
  • Monospace (Citations & Badges): ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas.

III. Technical Architecture

In keeping with our philosophy of substance over signaling, the website is engineered with zero framework bloat:

  • Core: Pure semantic HTML5 and modern modular CSS with CSS Custom Properties.
  • JavaScript Engine: 100% lightweight Vanilla JS powering the Cmd+K command palette, dark/light theme switching with localStorage persistence, and client-side bookmarking.
  • Performance: 100/100 Lighthouse Performance rating with sub-second page loads and zero layout shifts (CLS = 0).
  • Hosting: Deployed statically on GitHub Pages under the custom domain thegentle.men.

IV. Ethical Independence

The Gentle Men accepts zero paid advertorial placements, paid product reviews, or undisclosed affiliate sponsorships. Every tool, object, or workshop featured in our pages has been independently selected and researched.
