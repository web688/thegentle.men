Editorial Research Charter

Our 24-point mandatory protocol governing source verification, Claim Ledgers, primary archives, and factual integrity.

Standards Board Zero Commercial Retraction Tolerance

The Gentle Men is a research-driven publication. We operate under a strict, non-negotiable principle: Every substantive article must be built from primary and authoritative research before prose drafting begins. We strictly prohibit generating lifestyle copy and retrofitting citations after the fact.

1. The Mandatory 10-Stage Production Lifecycle

Every piece must pass sequentially through our 10-stage quality gate:

Question → Research → Evidence Assessment → Story Angle → Article Brief → Draft → Fact Check → Editorial Gate → Visual Brief & Imagery → Publication

2. The Four-Tier Source Hierarchy

Our research desk evaluates evidence strictly according to source independence and authority:

  • Tier 1 — Primary Sources (Preferred): Original historical manuscripts, patent filings, workshop blueprints, peer-reviewed datasets, and classical philosophical texts in authoritative translation.
  • Tier 2 — Authoritative Specialist Sources: Museum curators, accredited materials scientists, university presses, and master craft guild standards.
  • Tier 3 — High-Quality Secondary Journalism: Longform investigative reporting, broadsheet archives, and verified documentary accounts.
  • Tier 4 — Discovery Sources: Forums, Reddit, and commercial blogs. Used solely to identify leads or terminology, never as authoritative evidence.

3. The Claim Ledger and Verification Statuses

Before writing begins, every substantive factual assertion is logged in an internal Claim Ledger and assigned one of five explicit statuses:

  1. VERIFIED: Corroborated by independent primary or authoritative secondary evidence.
  2. VERIFIED WITH QUALIFICATION: Confirmed only under explicit conditions.
  3. CONTESTED: Credible authorities disagree; must be presented as an open debate.
  4. INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: Cannot be substantiated; excluded from factual assertions.
  5. REJECTED: Proven false or marketing exaggeration; excluded or explicitly debunked.

4. Zero Fabrication & Anti-Marketing Standards

We strictly forbid inventing experts, artisans, credentials, or interview quotes. Furthermore, luxury marketing copy is treated as what the manufacturer claims, not independent proof of truth.

"When The Gentle Men states something as fact, there is a definitive reason to trust it. Visual refinement must be matched by intellectual discipline."
